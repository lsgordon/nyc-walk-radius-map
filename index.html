How this works: walking routes are computed on OpenStreetMap sidewalks with the Valhalla router. Its speed is tuned to 4.3 km/h, about 2.7 mph, so its times match Google Maps, which runs about 10% slower than a generic 3 mph. Against 48 Google checks it's typically within ±0.5 min. The address checker times both directions to both offices and keeps the fastest. Anything near the line, confirm with the Google link before you sign.
